3. Interpretation of this gap

The checks establish the identities of the retained receipt and outputs, but
do not establish a complete chain from both frozen source files to the original
Farch execution. The mismatch alone does not prove a numerical error, absence
of execution, or invalidity of a reported comparison. Conversely, matching
output hashes do not authenticate the missing historical source versions.

The receipt's one-update equality and coverage fields describe the historical
diagnostic. This disclosure is a file-identity review, not a new execution of
that diagnostic. Farch remains a rolling-origin forward-discrepancy diagnostic
under an assumed replay law, not physical or causal validation of a mill.
Any future rerun must retain a separate, newly named receipt with its actual
source hashes, inputs, environment and execution status.

4. Frozen-weight replay is distinct from historical training reproduction

The released pipeline loads models/final_thickness_model.pt. It does not repeat
the original predictor fitting or recover its historical selection process.
As documented in TRAINING_AUDIT.md, the actual training update count, seed,
device, optimizer settings, source membership hashes, training curves and
original per-fold/per-seed LOPO score rows were not recovered. Published
aggregate scores and checkpoint metadata can be checked for consistency; that
does not reconstruct the historical fit. Defaults in later instrumented
training scripts are settings for a new experiment, not evidence of the budget
or seed used for the frozen checkpoint. An authorized replay rerun does not
close this training-provenance gap.

5. Public materials and confidential materials

The distributable materials comprise frozen model weights, code, versioned
configurations, de-identified input identities and permitted aggregate tables,
figures and audit summaries. Raw proprietary production CSV files are not
distributed. Production-derived row-level feature, prediction, state, command
and episode trajectories are also private, including row-level files generated
by an authorized replay. Changing a filename or removing a personal identifier
does not make such trajectories part of the public release. Published pass-,
condition- and batch-level summaries are aggregate evidence, not raw records.

6. Offline review and authorized full replay

Offline verification uses the distributed files to check their identities,
registered numerical identities, selected aggregate calculations and tested
implementation contracts. It does not reconstruct every result from production
records, reproduce historical training, or validate the emulator against a
physical mill.

Full replay requires lawful local access to all 26 authorized input CSV files
matching data_map/production_batch_map.csv. It executes the released replay
implementation using frozen weights, with the limitations disclosed here and
in README.md, DATA_ACCESS.md and TRAINING_AUDIT.md. Run it in a separate private
working copy so retained publication results are not overwritten. Generated
row-level outputs remain confidential and must not be included in a public
archive or commit. A new replay is evidence about the current execution; it is
not retroactive proof of the original source version or predictor training.
