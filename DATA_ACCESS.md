# Data access

Proprietary raw manufacturing records are confidential and are not redistributed.
The existing public materials are the frozen predictor weights, analysis code,
versioned configurations, de-identified input hashes, publication-level aggregate
tables, figures and audit summaries. This correction adds no raw production
records, feature windows or production-derived row-level trajectories.

An authorized replay requires legal access to all 26 original pass CSV files.
The files may be nested under a private root; `code/run_pipeline.py` locates them
by the SHA256 values in `data_map/production_batch_map.csv`. Private folder names
need not be published. The released pipeline loads the frozen model; it does not
recover historical fitting budgets, seeds, devices or fold-level training runs.

Run the full replay in a separate private working copy. Its feature, prediction,
state, command, episode and step-log trajectories remain production-derived
confidential material even though they are generated by public code. Do not
commit or upload those outputs. Only the previously approved de-identified
aggregate results and figure assets are retained in this public correction.

Without the raw archive, `python verify_results.py` and the public-statistics
audit check existing file identities and selected arithmetic, configuration and
implementation contracts. They do not regenerate every result from raw data,
reconstruct historical model training or validate a physical rolling mill.

`PROVENANCE_LIMITATIONS.txt` records two unmatched dependency hashes in the
original retained Farch audit. Its historical receipt is preserved unchanged;
new executions must have separate dated receipts and their actual source hashes.
